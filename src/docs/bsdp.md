As the Software Engineering Lead at the Molecular Evolution Core Laboratory
(MECL), I lead a team of five engineers in developing a comprehensive laboratory
information management system using Node.js, React.js, and MongoDB. This system
significantly enhanced sample tracking accuracy and streamlined workflows,
improving efficiency for lab technicians and clients. I designed and implemented
APIs, integrated third-party services, and introduced a CI/CD pipeline with
robust testing practices to ensure software reliability and reduce deployment
times. My role also involves conducting regular code reviews and feedback
sessions, fostering a collaborative environment that promotes continuous
improvement and innovation.

Recently, I have also led the expansion of our team of 5 developers into a team
of 14 developers, allowing us to work on more websites for each of the modules
of the Biotechnology Skills Development Program (BSDP). We are currently
developing websites for the entire MECL and our TipCycle service.

You can view our website [here](https://molevol.ibb.gatech.edu).
