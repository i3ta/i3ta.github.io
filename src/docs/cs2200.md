As an Undergraduate Teaching Assistant for CS 2200: Systems and Networks, I lead weekly recitation sessions for over 50
students, covering core topics such as processor architecture, instruction pipelines, virtual memory, operating systems,
and computer networking. I support students in applying theoretical concepts to practical problems, providing guidance
during debugging and helping them understand low-level systems behavior. During weekly office hours, I assist a class of
over 600 students by answering questions, troubleshooting code, and reinforcing course material. My role requires clear
communication, deep technical understanding, and the ability to break down complex system-level concepts into
approachable, intuitive explanations.
